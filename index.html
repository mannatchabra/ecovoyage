Eco tourism in India: questions people ask before booking

New to it? Read our complete guide to eco tourism in India.

What is eco tourism in India?

Eco tourism in India is travel to natural places that protects the environment and benefits local communities, such as village homestays in Spiti or community-run guesthouses in Meghalaya. On EcoVoyage, every stay is independently certified, so you can check the claim yourself.

Which are the best eco tourism places in India?

Popular eco tourism places in India include Coorg in Karnataka, the Kerala backwaters at Kumarakom, Spiti Valley in Himachal Pradesh, Mawlynnong in Meghalaya, Sikkim and Jawai in Rajasthan. All six have certified stays on EcoVoyage.

How do you verify that an eco resort in India is really eco-friendly?

We only list eco resorts and homestays certified by an independent body such as GSTC, Green Key or EarthCheck. Every listing names the certifier and the date of the last audit, and we remove stays whose certification lapses.

Is sustainable travel in India more expensive?

Often not. Our Starter Eco Escape in Coorg costs ₹14,900 for two nights with meals, guide and transfer included. The Green Receipt shows every rupee, so you can compare it with any regular booking.

What is a Green Receipt?

An itemised breakdown that comes with every booking. It shows who certified the stay, when it was audited, and how much of your money goes to the host, guides and community.

Is my trip carbon neutral?

No, and we will not claim it is. Travel has a footprint. We show exactly what your booking contributes, such as native saplings or trail restoration, and how that work is tracked.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Most stays offer free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. The exact policy is shown on each package before you pay.
