Proof, not promises

EcoVoyage makes sustainable travel something you can verify, not just believe. We connect travellers with independently certified stays and show exactly where every rupee goes, so choosing better takes less guesswork and less money than people assume.

Why we started

Most travel sites call a stay "eco" without saying who checked. Travellers who care end up doing hours of research, or giving up and booking a regular hotel.

Our vision is a travel industry where proof is the standard, not the premium. So we built the Green Receipt: an itemised record of every booking that anyone can read.

Our commitments

  • Every listing shows its audit dateIf a certification lapses, the stay comes off EcoVoyage until it is renewed.
  • Every rupee is itemisedThe Green Receipt shows what goes to hosts, guides, communities and to us.
  • We never claim "carbon neutral"Travel has a footprint. We show what your trip contributes and how it is tracked.

Certifications we accept

We only list stays audited by independent bodies. Self-declared badges do not count.

GSTC

The Global Sustainable Tourism Council sets the baseline criteria that most credible eco labels are measured against.

Green Key

An international label for hotels and homestays covering energy, water, waste and staff practices.

EarthCheck

A science-based certification that benchmarks a property's environmental performance year on year.

India's national criteria

We also track alignment with the Sustainable Tourism Criteria for India (STCI) and the Travel for LiFE programme.
